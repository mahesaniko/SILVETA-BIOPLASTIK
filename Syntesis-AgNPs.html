Syntesis AgNPs.

Exploring two approaches to silver nanoparticle synthesis: the conventional method and green synthesis using natural plant extracts.

TWO SYNTHESIS APPROACHES
01 CHEMICAL APPROACH
CONVENTIONAL METHOD

Conventional Method

The conventional method uses chemical reducing agents to convert silver ions (Ag⁺) into metallic silver (Ag⁰), enabling the formation of silver nanoparticles (AgNPs).

⚗

How does it work?

A chemical reducing agent, such as sodium borohydride (NaBH₄), can facilitate the reduction process under suitable reaction conditions.

02 NATURAL APPROACH
GREEN SYNTHESIS

Green Synthesis

Green synthesis explores the use of plant extracts as a potential alternative in silver nanoparticle synthesis. Natural compounds may contribute to reduction and particle stabilization.

🌿

How does it work?

Plant extracts, including betel leaf extract, contain natural compounds that may support nanoparticle formation depending on the experimental conditions.
