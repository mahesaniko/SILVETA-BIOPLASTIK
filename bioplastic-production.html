SILVETA · RESEARCH PROCESS 03

Bioplastic Production

From natural materials to biodegradable plastic through a simple laboratory process.

THE INGREDIENTS

Materials Required

Basic materials for a small experimental bioplastic batch.

♧

Starch

Acts as the main polymer source for forming the bioplastic film.

◉

Water

Helps disperse the starch before the heating process.

❋

Glycerol

Acts as a plasticizer to help improve the flexibility of the film.

✳️

Optional Additive

Can be included to investigate additional material properties.

✓

Important Note

Ingredient ratios and heating conditions can affect the final result. Record the experimental conditions for every trial and follow laboratory safety procedures.

THE METHOD

Production Steps

From mixing to the finished film.

  1. 01

    Mixing the Materials

    Mix tapioka starch, water, glycerol, and selected ingredients untoil evenly combined.

  2. 02

    Film Casting

    Heat and stir the mixture until uniform, then pour it into a mold.

  3. 03

    Drying

    Dry the cast mixture in an oven under controlled conditions to form a bioplastic film.

  4. 04

    Finishing

    Remove the dried film and evaluate its apperance, flexibility, and structure.
